01 / The vision

Not just a lounge.
A cultural address.

NOIR 713 is envisioned as a refined social destination where the energy of Houston nightlife meets the intimacy of a boutique hospitality experience.

Obsidian finishes, oxblood velvet, warm gold lighting, thoughtfully designed seating, and a music-forward atmosphere define the brand.

Private experiences
